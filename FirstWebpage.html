My trip to Glacier National Park

GNP is huge!

Glacier National Park spans over 1 million acres and sees 318+ million visitors every year! Glacier is roughly the size of the state of Rhode Island. There are 750 miles of hiking trails throughout the park.

GNP is gorgeours!

I got to see so many wonderful views while in the park! From wildflowers to Big Horn Sheep, I was in awe the entire time. I am so lucky to have been able to take a trip to the park this summer.
